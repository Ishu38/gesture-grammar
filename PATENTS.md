# Patent Notice and Grant

## The patent

The MLAF system embodied in this repository is the subject of:

- **Indian patent application no. 202631020540**, filed 2026, 10 claims.
- Inventor and applicant: **Neil Shankar Ray**.
- Status: pending (under examination by the Indian Patent Office).
- International filing: PCT application planned on or before 21 February 2027.

The application discloses, among other things, a bidirectional abductive
feedback loop between symbolic grammar reasoning and neural perception
thresholds; a four-layer in-browser Graph RAG over a typed knowledge graph;
trimodal (visual + acoustic + gaze) Bayesian gesture fusion; and the use of
typed lambda calculus to issue compositional-generalization proof
certificates for novel sentence formation. The full claim set is on file
with the Indian Patent Office and will become public in due course.
